# EduVerify AI
**Tagline:** Securing Academic Credentials, One Certificate at a Time.

## 🚨 The Problem
Fake degrees cost the global economy millions and severely damage institutional reputation. Current verification methods are heavily manual, slow, and prone to human error, allowing sophisticated forgeries to slip through HR background checks.

## 💡 The Solution
EduVerify AI is an automated, multi-layered authenticity validator. By combining AI-powered Optical Character Recognition (OCR) with cryptographic perceptual hashing, the platform instantly cross-references uploaded certificates against an immutable institutional database to detect even the slightest tampering.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Tamper Detection:** Automatically highlights the exact fields (e.g., marks, name, year) that were altered on a forged certificate.
- **Enterprise Dashboard:** A centralized fraud intelligence hub for administrators to monitor verification trends and manage blacklisted actors.
- **One-Click Reporting:** Instantly generates cryptographically signed PDF compliance reports and permanent verification QR codes.
- **Global Blacklist:** A pre-validation hook that instantly blocks known fraudulent certificates from being processed, saving compute resources.
- **Visual Watermarking:** Embeds an anti-forgery visual watermark onto digital copies of verified certificates.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** React.js, TailwindCSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** SQLite3
- **Core Engines:** Tesseract.js (OCR), Image-Hash (Perceptual Hashing), Sharp (Image Processing)

## 🚀 The Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Current):** Standalone web platform MVP with OCR and Hashing.
- **Phase 2 (Next 6 Months):** B2B REST APIs for direct integration into university issuance systems and corporate HR platforms (Workday, SAP).
- **Phase 3 (Future):** Transitioning the centralized SQLite database to a decentralized Blockchain ledger for immutable, zero-trust verification.
